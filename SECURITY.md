## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you find a security vulnerability, please do not report it publicly through the public issue tracker. Instead, follow the steps below:

1. **Email us privately:** Send a detailed report to [your-security-email@example.com](mailto:abdallamsema69@gmail.com).
2. **Include details:** Describe the vulnerability, how to reproduce it, and any potential impact or exploits.
3. **Wait for a response:** We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and provide a timeline for a fix.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before making any information public.
